For parents and teachers

Help your explorer thrive

Code Quest is designed for new computer learners at about a fifth-grade reading level. No account or internet connection is needed.

⏱

Time

About 5–10 minutes per mission. Short breaks between levels work well.

🧠

Skills

Sequencing, debugging, AI checking, patterns, loops, functions, parameters, and explaining ideas.

🔒

Privacy

Progress stays in this browser on this device. No learner information is collected or sent.

💬

How to help

Ask “What did you notice?” before giving an answer. Hints are part of learning—not a penalty.

Try asking: “What did you expect?” “What happened?” “What is one thing you could change?”
